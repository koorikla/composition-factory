**Oracle clause — the password never appears as a literal: met.** Every occurrence across all


**Oracle clause — artifacts carry the connection-secret envelope: not reached.** Neither the

than wrong: this starter does not claim the feature — its tags are `Cloud-Agnostic`,
`Database`, `PostgreSQL`, `Kubernetes`, `StatefulSet`, `Environment`, with no `Envelopes`
tag, unlike the RDS card. The envelope route runs through the RDS starter, which #68 blocks
here. **Where the missing schemas stopped the mission:** clause 1 of M3's oracle. It needs a
seeded `provider-aws-rds` cache, or any starter tagged `Envelopes` whose kinds are native.
